LOFTY is alive.

LOFTY is a premium AI assistant project built to feel human, cinematic, and usable. It combines a bold visual identity with voice interaction, tool-based actions, memory, and a polished desktop-style experience.

The goal is simple: make the assistant feel present from the first frame. The design uses a deep black background, refined typography, a green brand accent, and motion-led storytelling so the experience feels modern and memorable.
